How I work

Four deliberate choices that remove friction and give people ownership.

  1. 01

    One standup, one topic.

    Instead of a ticket-by-ticket status round, each day's standup is about the single most important thing for the team right now. Meetings stay short, and everyone leaves knowing what matters today.

  2. 02

    Under half a day? Just do it.

    Small fixes, tech debt and improvements don't need a ticket, a refinement session or permission. That creates flow and ownership, and the platform keeps getting better on its own.

  3. 03

    Work in twos and threes.

    When a task needs it, people group up in pairs or threes. Knowledge spreads naturally, and the team builds a shared view of how we build things, without relying on documentation.

  4. 04

    Agentic by default.

    I introduce AI by example: start with concrete, repetitive tasks like "build a UI component to our standards, with tests", then move to agentic frameworks that handle the orchestration.
